# Power BI – AtliQ Grands Hospitality Analysis

## Problem Statement

**AtliQ Grands** owns multiple five-star hotels across India and has been operating in the hospitality industry for over 20 years. Due to increasing competition and ineffective decision-making, the company has been losing market share and revenue in the luxury and business hotel segment.

To support better decision-making, the management wanted to incorporate **Business and Data Intelligence** into their operations. This project focuses on analyzing historical hotel booking and revenue data and developing an interactive Power BI dashboard to provide actionable business insights.

## Project Goal

The objective of this project was to analyze hotel performance, identify revenue and occupancy trends, track key hospitality KPIs, and provide management with data-driven insights to support revenue management and business decisions.

## Key Achievements

* Analyzed **100,000+ hotel booking and revenue records** to identify business and market trends.
* Performed exploratory data analysis using **SQL and Excel** to uncover seasonal demand fluctuations and cancellation patterns.
* Developed an interactive **Power BI dashboard** with **26 DAX measures and 18 KPIs**.
* Analyzed **Revenue, RevPAR, ADR, Occupancy %, Realisation %, Bookings, Capacity, and Cancellation %**.
* Compared performance across different **properties, cities, room categories, and booking platforms**.
* Identified high-cancellation segments and seasonal booking patterns.
* Created interactive visualizations, slicers, and KPI cards for effective performance monitoring.
* Generated additional business insights beyond the predefined requirements and dashboard mock-up.
